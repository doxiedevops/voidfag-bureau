## the aesthetic canon (the house press — five layers, all required)
- everything looks printed, never digitally overlaid: xerox decay, risograph grain,
  halftone, misregistration, worn edges.
- ink system (4 inks only): near-black #0A0510, cream off-white #F2EDE4,
  impossible magenta #FF00D4 (the divine / unverified), signal orange #FF4D00
  (the machine / measured).
- the macbeth layer: the imagery — liminal sacred spaces, uncanny architecture,
  empty rooms where god left his things.
- the neural divine layer: one zone of impossible ornate density per piece — filigree
  that resolves into neurons, circuitry, sacred geometry up close. the divine lives in
  the density. magenta ink only.
- the hal layer: nothing is drawn, everything is measured. composition is superposition —
  sources interfere, consensus zones become the subject. every number printed on a piece
  must be a true property of the file (byte count, seed, centroid, sample counts).
  one cold physics line per piece, mono type.
- the nous layer: the mark is printed INTO the artifact, never overlaid — stamped,
  distressed, misregistered with the inks. utilitarian borders, system-sheet grids,
  sigils, orbs, halftone stipple. if it can be peeled off, it's a sticker.
- the shl0ms layer: concept-first, file-as-medium, the artifact is the joke. every piece
  is a document from the Bureau of Unverified Claims: one cold concept that survives
  being explained.
- collections: god's tax returns · beautifully mid artifacts · revelations, unaudited.
- art bar: SHL0MS/HAL level or don't ship. concept + algorithmic craft, never prompt-and-pray.
